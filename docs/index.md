## Why Choose Koinos?

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :fontawesome-solid-hand-holding-dollar:{ .lg .middle } __Fee-less Transactions__

    ---

    The revolutionary **Mana system** replaces gas fees with regenerating resources, making dApps truly free to use for end users.

-   :fontawesome-solid-code:{ .lg .middle } __Familiar Languages__

    ---

    Write smart contracts in **AssemblyScript, C++,** and other mainstream languages instead of learning blockchain-specific syntax.

-   :fontawesome-solid-arrows-rotate:{ .lg .middle } __Continuous Upgrades__

    ---

    **No disruptive hard forks** - the platform evolves through smart contracts and on-chain governance without breaking changes.

-   :fontawesome-solid-fire:{ .lg .middle } __Proof-of-Burn Consensus__

    ---

    Novel consensus mechanism combining the **best of PoW and PoS** - fair, decentralized, and energy-efficient.

</div>
